# Dawn: Prayer Times & Qibla — Support

Dawn is a simple, beautiful prayer times app for iPhone and Apple Watch: accurate salah times computed on your device, a qibla compass, a tasbih counter, gentle reminders, and Home Screen widgets. Everything works offline and every feature is free.

## Get help

- **Questions, bug reports, or feature requests**: [open an issue](https://github.com/alyakan/dawn-app-site/issues) and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**Why do the times differ slightly from my mosque?**
Dawn automatically picks the calculation convention most common in your region. If your mosque follows a different one, change it in Settings → Advanced.

**Does the app work without location access?**
Yes — choose any city manually. Location, when granted, is used only on your device to compute times and never leaves it.

**Is anything paid or locked?**
No. Every feature is free, and there are no ads of any kind. If you'd like to support development there's an optional tip jar — "Support Development" on the Today screen — with a few one-off amounts. Tipping unlocks nothing; it's a thank-you, not a purchase.
